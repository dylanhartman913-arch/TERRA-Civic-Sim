How the scores are built: 1,676 census tracts across 9 Mountain West states are scored on 16 indicators. Each indicator is min-max normalized to 0–10 using the Mountain West distribution (not national, preserving regional variation). Indicators within each capital type are averaged into a capital score. Ecoregion scores are population-weighted averages across constituent tracts. Composite score = mean(E, Ec, S).

Data vintage: ACS 5-year 2022 · TIGER/Line 2020 · EIA power plants 2024 · EPA Level III ecoregions
